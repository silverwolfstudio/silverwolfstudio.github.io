Privacy policy

Without an account, everything stays on your iPhone. With an account, only what shared homes need goes to our server in the EU. No ads, no tracking.

Who we are

Better safe than sorry is made by Silver Wolf Studio. For anything related to your data, write to silverwolfstudiodev@gmail.com.

On your iPhone

Your checks, scenarios, history, photos and videos, notes, tasks and settings are saved only on this iPhone, inside the app. We can’t see them and never receive them, with or without an account.

Optional account

You only need an account for shared homes and friends. We then store your email address (or the anonymous identifier from Sign in with Apple, which can hide your real address), the name you choose and your friend code. We use them only for the features you choose.

Shared homes and friends

For a shared home we store its name and icon, the place you put it on the map, its members and settings, the invitations you send (with the invitee’s email address), who’s home, and who checked what and when. Only the home’s members see this. Friends see only the home’s name and an approximate area about 1 km wide — never who’s home or what was checked.

Location

Used on your iPhone to show the map and to place your home, only after you allow it. Only the home location you save is sent to the server; where you are is never sent or stored.

Where the data is kept

On Supabase servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU), sent over an encrypted connection. Supabase processes the data only on our behalf, under a data processing agreement. We keep it as long as your account exists.

No tracking, no ads

No usage analytics, no ads, no profiling. We don’t sell your data and don’t share it with other companies, apart from the server provider above.

Camera and photos

Used only when you add a photo or video to a check or a task. The files stay on your iPhone and aren’t uploaded anywhere, not even for shared homes.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Face ID

Handled entirely by iOS. The app only learns whether unlocking worked — never anything about your face.

iCloud Backup

If you use iCloud Backup, iOS includes the app’s data in your iPhone backup, in your Apple account.

Deleting your data

Deleting your account (Settings → Account → Delete account) removes it from the server right away, together with your homes and everything linked to them. Settings → Data & storage → Delete all data removes everything on this iPhone; deleting the app does the same.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how it’s used. Write to us and we’ll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

This website

The website uses no cookies and no analytics. It’s hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address in its server logs, for security.

Waiting list

If you join the waiting list, we store your email address, the site language and when you agreed. We use them only to send you one email when the app launches, then delete them. They’re kept on our Supabase server in the EU, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to us at silverwolfstudiodev@gmail.com.
