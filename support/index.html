Frequently asked questions

Reminders don’t arrive

Open Settings → Notifications in the app. If notifications are off, you’ll see a red warning with a button to turn them on. Also check that a Focus mode or Do Not Disturb isn’t silencing the app.

How does “press and hold” work?

On Home, press and hold the round button: your checks tick off one by one while you hold. Lift your finger early and nothing gets checked.

Can I require a photo?

Yes. Press and hold a check → Photo → Required. Or turn on “Check only with a photo” for all checks in Settings → Checks.

Do I need an account?

No. An account is only needed for shared homes and friends. Everything else works without one, and your data stays on your iPhone.

How do shared homes work?

Create a shared scenario on Home, place the home on the map and invite people with a code or by email. Members see who checked what and who’s home; friends only see an approximate area.

How do I delete my account or my data?

In the app, Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account and its server data right away. Settings → Data & storage → Delete all data removes everything on your iPhone. You can also write to us.

Which devices are supported?

The app is made for iPhone.

Still need help?

Write to us and tell us what happened, ideally with your iPhone model and iOS version.
